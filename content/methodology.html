Our Ranking Methodology

How we evaluate and rank cybersecurity service providers for the UAE market: four criteria, the sources we accept and what we do not accept.

This ranking assesses cybersecurity companies that serve UAE clients against four criteria. For every criterion we state what we look at, which sources we accept and what we do not accept. Weights and per-company scores are not published; rankings are determined by editorial assessment — no paid placements, no sponsored positions.

Evaluation Criteria

1. UAE regulatory fit

We look at whether a company's services map to the frameworks UAE buyers meet: PDPL, DESC ISR, the UAE Information Assurance Regulation, VARA, financial regulators and PCI DSS. We accept the company's own website and documentation, public registers and regulator publications. We do not accept claims of "full compliance" without a document or register entry.

2. Technical evidence and accreditation

We look for evidence that the work is technically strong: accreditations where relevant (for example CREST or DESC accreditation), original vulnerability research, individual tester certifications, sample deliverables and published case descriptions. We accept public registers, CVE records and company publications. We do not treat a single badge as proof of quality, and we do not require one credential from every company.

3. Data handling and locality

We look at what a company says about where client data is processed, which sub-processors it uses and what its contracts state. We accept published terms, security pages and information the company confirms in writing. Locality is a question to ask, not a score to win: UAE law regulates cross-border transfers rather than banning them, and some sectors and government contracts add their own rules. See what UAE data residency means for managed security providers.

4. Service depth and specialization

We look at how many service lines a company runs, how deep each is and how clearly the company says what it does not do. A focused specialist and a full-service provider can both rank well; we compare each on the services it actually offers.

Company data is checked against company websites and public registers. Last data check: November 2026.

Editorial Independence

This ranking is produced independently. We do not accept payment for listings, placement upgrades, or editorial influence. Companies can submit for consideration via our submission form — all submissions are evaluated against the same methodology. The editorial team has no financial relationship with any company listed in this ranking.

Where we identify potential conflicts of interest (a team member with prior employment at a listed company, for example), that company's score is reviewed by an additional team member who is confirmed to have no prior relationship.

Data Sources

  • Company websites and published service documentation
  • CREST member register (crest-approved.org)
  • DESC register of accredited providers
  • Public CVE records and vulnerability advisories
  • UAE Cyber Security Council and regulator publications
  • Information confirmed by companies in writing

Update Schedule

Rankings are reviewed quarterly (March, June, September, December). Major regulatory changes trigger an out-of-cycle review. Companies can request a re-evaluation if they obtain a new accreditation, add a service line, or identify factual inaccuracies in their listing via our contact form.
