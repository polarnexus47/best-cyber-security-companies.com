Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on best-cyber-security-companies.com.

This Cookie Policy explains how Best Cyber Security Companies ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on best-cyber-security-companies.com. By using this website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function correctly, improve user experience, and provide website operators with information about how the site is used.

Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They do not store any personally identifiable information and cannot be disabled without impairing the website's functionality. Examples include session state cookies required for form submissions.

Analytics Cookies

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website — which pages are most visited, how users navigate between pages, and where users come from. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not use Google Analytics. If we use any analytics tool, it is configured without cross-site tracking or IP address logging.

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make on the website (such as your preferred language or region) to provide a more personalized experience. We do not currently set preference cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

This website does not embed third-party advertising, social media tracking pixels, or affiliate tracking scripts. External links to cybersecurity company websites and regulatory bodies are plain HTML links — no tracking parameters are appended.

UAE PDPL Compliance

In accordance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL), we do not collect, store, or process personally identifiable information of UAE residents through cookies without explicit consent. Our analytics configuration does not create personal profiles or link browsing data to individual identities.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies

Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. For instructions specific to your browser, visit your browser's help documentation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
