About This Ranking

An independent editorial resource for CISOs and IT decision-makers evaluating cybersecurity vendors in the UAE.

Best Cyber Security Companies is an independent editorial ranking of cybersecurity service providers operating in the UAE and broader MENA region. The site exists to help CISOs, IT directors, and procurement leads make informed decisions about cybersecurity vendor selection in a market where regulatory requirements, local presence, and service depth vary significantly between providers.

Who This Resource Is For

The primary audience is security decision-makers at organizations based in or operating within the UAE. This includes:

  • CISOs and IT Directors at UAE enterprises, financial institutions, and government-adjacent entities
  • Procurement teams evaluating cybersecurity vendors for RFP shortlisting
  • Compliance officers assessing DESC ISR V3, NESA, PDPL, and VARA vendor requirements
  • Founders and CTOs at UAE startups building their first security program
  • Virtual asset service providers (VASPs) subject to VARA cybersecurity requirements

Our Editorial Team

The ranking is produced by a team of cybersecurity practitioners and compliance specialists with direct experience in UAE and GCC security operations, penetration testing, and regulatory advisory. Team members have backgrounds in managed security services, offensive security, and financial sector compliance.

We do not disclose individual team member identities to protect editorial independence and prevent vendor lobbying directed at specific reviewers. Our methodology is fully documented and publicly available at our methodology page.

What We Are Not

We are not a cybersecurity vendor, reseller, or affiliate. We do not earn commission or referral fees from any company listed in this ranking. We do not operate a lead generation service on behalf of any vendor. Companies cannot purchase a ranking position, an "editor's choice" badge, or any other form of editorial influence.

We are also not a certification body. Our rankings represent editorial assessment against published criteria — they do not constitute regulatory approval, government endorsement, or professional certification of any kind.

Contact and Corrections

If you identify a factual error in a company listing, wish to submit a company for consideration, or have questions about our methodology, please use our contact form. We aim to respond to editorial queries within 5 business days.
